# NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE: REALITY FIRST

KisaanBuddy must be a completely authentic agricultural platform.

Never sacrifice authenticity to make the UI look populated, attractive or functional.

---

## STRICTLY PROHIBITED

* AI-generated product photographs.
* Fake product listings.
* Dummy seller accounts.
* Fabricated prices.
* Invented specifications.
* Fake customer reviews.
* Fake marketplace activity.
* Simulated transactions presented as real.
* Unverified agricultural information.
* Placeholder data presented as genuine data.
* Random images assigned to products.

---

## AUTHENTIC DATA POLICY

1. **Genuine Source**: Every product must have a genuine, identifiable source.
2. **Accurate Imagery**: Every product image must correspond to the actual product or exact model.
3. **Real Sellers**: Every seller must represent a real seller.
4. **Verified Pricing**: Every price must come from a genuine listing or verified source.
5. **Traceable Specifications**: Every technical specification must have a traceable source.
6. **Provenance & Timestamps**: Every external data point must have its source and last-updated timestamp wherever applicable.
7. **Honest Verification Claims**: Never claim information is verified unless verification actually happened.

---

## MISSING DATA POLICY

When authentic information is unavailable:
* Do not invent it.
* Do not use random substitutes.
* Do not generate an artificial replacement.
* Clearly indicate that verified information is unavailable.
* Do not represent incomplete reference data as a live commercial listing.

---

## MARKETPLACE-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT

Strictly distinguish between:
* **A. Reference catalogue products** (e.g. government testing/manufacturer specs, indicative benchmarks).
* **B. Actual products listed by real sellers for rent or sale** (verified farmer/owner inventory).

A reference machinery model must never automatically be represented as an available commercial listing.

---

## FINAL RULE

**Authenticity is more important than visual completeness.**

It is better to show fewer genuine products than hundreds of misleading listings.

Apply this policy across the entire KisaanBuddy application, including frontend, backend, APIs, databases, AI features, and external integrations.
