# KisaanBuddy — Image Sources and Licensing Registry

This document records the provenance, authorship, licensing, and intended application for all photographic assets integrated into the KisaanBuddy platform.

## Policy & Licensing Compliance
- **Zero AI-Generated Visuals**: No synthetic avatars, cartoonish characters, or AI hallucinated imagery.
- **Authentic Indian Agriculture**: Real farming conditions in India across smallholder crop production, APMC mandis, agronomic soil sampling, and disease inspection.
- **Licensing Compliance**: All assets originate from verified public copyright licenses (Creative Commons CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0, or CC BY 3.0) and are stored locally to prevent external link dependency and privacy leaks.
