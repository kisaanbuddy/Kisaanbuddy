## Integrated nutrient management and soil fertility
TNAU describes integrated nutrient management as coordinating organic, mineral and biological nutrient sources to maintain soil fertility and crop nutrient supply. Its decision factors include the cropping system's needs, soil fertility, locally available nutrient resources, farmer economics and environmental considerations.

Scope: general principles, not a fertilizer prescription. The excerpt supplies no application quantities, product mixtures or schedule. Soil results and local crop-specific guidance are needed for a field recommendation.
