## Crop rotation and seasonal crop selection
TNAU describes alternating legumes and non-legumes in crop sequences. Its examples include green gram followed by wheat or maize. It also describes following long-duration crops with seasonal or restorative crops and rotating crops with different rooting depths.

Scope: general rotation principles from a Tamil Nadu agricultural university resource, not a district-specific sowing calendar or a suitability assessment for an individual field. Obtain location, previous crop, season and irrigation context before applying these principles. No yield, price or chemical-dose claim is included in this excerpt.
